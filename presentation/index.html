OVERVIEW

What is the F-35 Lightning II?

The F-35 Lightning II is a fifth-generation, multirole stealth fighter developed by Lockheed Martin for the United States and allied nations.

"Multirole" means a single airframe can execute many different mission types.

Mission Capabilities

  • Air superiority & airspace protection
  • Precision strike on ground targets
  • Intelligence, Surveillance & Reconnaissance
  • Close air support for friendly forces

Program Timeline

Operated by the United States and a growing coalition of NATO allies and international partners.

LOW OBSERVABILITY

Stealth Technology

Low observability is central to the F-35's survivability. The aircraft is engineered to minimise detection across multiple spectra.

  • ◆ Faceted airframe geometry reduces radar cross-section
  • ◆ Radar-absorbent materials (RAM) coat critical surfaces
  • ◆ Internal weapons bays eliminate external stores

Result: A drastically reduced radar signature — the F-35 is extremely difficult for adversary systems to detect, track, or engage.

AVIONICS

A Flying Supercomputer

The F-35's mission systems process more data than any fighter in history, giving pilots an unprecedented decision-making advantage.

AN/APG-81 AESA Radar

Simultaneously detects, tracks, and engages air and ground targets at extended range.

AN/AAQ-37 DAS

Six infrared sensors provide a seamless 360-degree spherical view around the aircraft.

Gen III HMDS

Helmet-Mounted Display System projects flight data, targeting, and sensor imagery directly onto the visor.

Sensor Fusion Engine

Correlates data from every onboard and offboard sensor into a single unified tactical picture.

Conclusion

The F-35 Lightning II represents a generational leap in combat aviation, integrating:

  • Low-observable stealth design
  • Advanced sensor fusion and networking
  • Multi-spectrum situational awareness
  • Interoperability across allied forces
